# High-End Art Direction and Asset Generation Guide

## Shared visual standard

All artwork should feel intentionally commissioned for one premium product.

Avoid:
- clipart
- sticker collages
- generic vector packs
- random stock imagery
- inconsistent illustration styles
- overly saturated children's-game palettes
- text embedded inside generated images

Prefer:
- cinematic storybook environments
- editorial children's illustration
- premium animated-film concept-art quality
- believable lighting and depth
- controlled negative space for UI
- edge-weighted composition so content remains readable

## Background master prompt pattern

"Premium [THEME] environment for a high-end parenting mobile app, vertical 9:16 composition, sophisticated storybook realism, layered depth, cinematic natural lighting, important characters and decorative elements concentrated around edges and corners, generous calm negative space through the central UI column, no text, no logos, no interface elements, visually rich but not cluttered, consistent family-friendly art direction, professional animated-film concept-art quality."

Recommended master size: 1536x2732, export optimized WebP variants after cropping tests.

## Hero master prompt pattern

"Wide editorial [THEME] scene for a premium parenting app hero card, 16:9 composition, deliberate subject placement away from the primary text zone, elegant depth and lighting, polished storybook realism, no text, no logos, no UI, coherent with the matching page background."

Recommended master size: 1792x1024.

## Tile artwork

Generate or illustrate tile subjects on transparent backgrounds where possible. Keep a consistent camera angle, lighting model and rendering style across each theme family.

Tile masters: 768x768 transparent PNG/WebP, later downsample for runtime.

## Individual worlds

### Animal Kingdom
Lush jungle, giraffe, elephant, lion cub, tiger cub, monkey, parrots; deep botanical sophistication for Mom.

### Unicorn Dream
Cloud kingdoms, elegant unicorns, soft moon/stars, restrained rainbow light; avoid candy-pink overload.

### Butterfly Garden
Botanical meadow/garden, realistic elegant butterflies, wildflowers, warm daylight; refined rather than nursery clipart.

### Ocean
Whales, sea turtles, reef fish, coral, volumetric water light; deep teal editorial Mom variant.

### Moon & Stars
Moonlit clouds, constellations, soft planetary/celestial motifs; naturally strong dark-mode identity.

### Woodland
Deer, fox, rabbit, bear, mushrooms, moss, filtered forest light; tactile and calm.

### Safari Sunset
Golden savanna, giraffe, zebra, elephant, acacia silhouettes, sunset haze; warm sophisticated earth palette.

### Floral Meadow
Refined seasonal meadow with bees/butterflies/flowers; broad negative space and soft morning light.

### Cozy Clouds
Minimal premium sky world; dimensional clouds, subtle stars, sunrise/sunset light; the quietest theme for users who prefer less visual density.
